    Before you apply

    Essential tips to speed up your loan sanction and negotiate the lowest possible rate.

    • Check credit score (750+ secures the lowest rates).
    • Review the total cost including processing and prepayment fees.
    • Ensure proposed EMI does not exceed 50% of monthly income.

    Identity and address proof (Aadhaar, PAN), last 3 months’ salary slips, last 6 months’ salary account statements, and latest Form 16 / ITR returns.

    Identity and address proof, business registration (GST, MSME Udyam, COI), last 2 to 3 years’ ITR with audited financials, and 6 to 12 months’ current account statements.

    Registered title deed, chain of prior deeds, approved building plan, latest property tax receipts, and encumbrance certificate, along with income and KYC proofs.
