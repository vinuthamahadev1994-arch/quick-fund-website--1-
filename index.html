    Before you apply

    A little preparation can speed up approval and help you get a better rate.

    • Check your credit score. 750 or above usually gets better offers.
    • Look at the total cost, including processing fee and prepayment charges.
    • Borrow only what you can comfortably repay each month.

    Identity and address proof (Aadhaar, PAN), last 3 months’ salary slips, last 6 months’ account statements, and Form 16 or recent ITR.

    Identity and address proof, business registration or GST certificate, last 2 years’ ITR with financial statements, and 6 to 12 months’ account statements.

    Property papers such as the sale deed, title documents, tax receipts and approved plan, along with your identity, income and account statements.
