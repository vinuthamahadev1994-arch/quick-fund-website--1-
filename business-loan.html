Who can apply

A business running for 2 or more years with turnover proof, GST registration and bank statements.

Documents you may need

  • Identity and address proof of promoters/directors (Aadhaar, PAN)
  • Business registration proof (GST certificate, Udyam MSME, Partnership deed/COI)
  • Last 2 to 3 years’ audited financial statements with ITR returns
  • Last 6 to 12 months’ current bank account statements

Before you apply

  • Check your credit score. 750+ unlocks lowest interest slabs.
  • Compare processing fees and prepayment charges alongside rates.
  • Ask if the rate is fixed or linked to the repo benchmark.
  • Borrow within your comfortable monthly repayment threshold.
